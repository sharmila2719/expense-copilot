# Acme Corp — Employee Expense & Reimbursement Policy

This policy governs business expenses submitted for reimbursement. Expense
Copilot retrieves the relevant clauses below when evaluating each receipt.

## General Rules
All business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and incurred for a valid
business purpose. Employees should choose cost-effective options. An itemized
receipt is required for any single expense at or above USD 25. Expenses without
the required receipt will be flagged for manual review.

## Meals & Entertainment
Individual meals are reimbursable up to a limit of USD 75 per person per day.
Business meals with clients are reimbursable up to USD 150 per person, provided
attendees and business purpose are noted. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part
of a client business meal. Meal expenses above the limit require manager
approval and will be flagged.

## Travel
Airfare should be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours. The travel
category cap for a single domestic flight is USD 650. Business class is only
permitted for flights over 8 hours and requires prior manager approval.
Flights above the cap will be flagged for approval.

## Lodging
Hotel lodging is reimbursable up to a limit of USD 250 per night. Stays above
this nightly limit require manager approval and will be flagged. Room service
and in-room entertainment (movies, minibar) are not reimbursable and will be
rejected.

## Transportation
Ground transportation such as taxis, rideshare, and rental cars is reimbursable
up to USD 100 per trip. Standard or economy rental car classes should be chosen.
Daily parking is reimbursable up to USD 40 per day.

## Software & Subscriptions
Software and SaaS subscriptions for business use are reimbursable up to USD 500
per purchase. Purchases above this limit require manager approval. Personal
subscriptions are not reimbursable.

## Office Supplies
Office supplies are reimbursable up to USD 200 per purchase without approval.

## Prohibited and Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following are prohibited and will be rejected: personal entertainment,
traffic or parking fines, personal travel, gift cards, and personal
subscriptions. These categories are never reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Approval Thresholds
Any single expense above USD 1,000 requires director-level approval regardless
of category and will be flagged for review.
